🚜 Harvest Hollow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

The short version:

  • We only store what the game needs: your Discord user ID, the server ID, and your game progress.
  • Harvest Hollow doesn't read your messages. It only sees the slash commands you use.
  • Discord handles Premium payments. We never see your card or payment details.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone for marketing.
  • Email us any time to get your data deleted.

1. Who we are

Harvest Hollow is run by Joshua Rosario, based in New York, New York, USA ("we", "us"). If you have questions about your data, email joshuarosario84@outlook.com.

2. What we store

When you use Harvest Hollow in a server, we store:

  • IDs: your Discord user ID and the ID of the server you used Harvest Hollow in.
  • Game data: your coins, farm plots, what's planted and when, your barn's crops and fish, and cooldown timers.

Only game data is stored. We don't store your messages, email address, phone number or IP address.

3. What Discord shares with us

Each time you use a command, Discord sends Harvest Hollow what it needs to respond: your user ID, username, display name and avatar, the server and channel you're in, and whether you have an active Harvest Hollow Premium subscription. We use this to run the command and show your name and avatar in replies. Apart from the IDs and game data listed above, we don't store any of it.

Harvest Hollow doesn't use Discord's message content access, so it can't read your normal messages.

4. Payments

Harvest Hollow Premium is bought through Discord. Discord processes the payment under its own Privacy Policy. All we receive is a record saying your account has an active subscription, which we use to turn on Premium perks. We never see your card number, billing address or other payment details.

5. How we use your data

  • to run the game, including leaderboards,
  • to give Premium perks to subscribers,
  • to fix bugs and stop cheating, using short-lived technical logs from our hosting provider.

We don't sell your data, use it for ads, or build profiles of you.

6. Who can see it

Other members of a server can see your farm, coins and leaderboard rank in that server through commands like /farm and /leaderboard. Your data in one server isn't shown in any other server.

Our data is stored with the company that hosts the bot's server (for example Railway or a similar provider). They only store and run it for us. We'd only share your data with anyone else if the law requires it.

7. How long we keep it

We keep game data while Harvest Hollow is running so your progress isn't lost. If a server removes Harvest Hollow, its data stays in case they add it back, unless the owner asks us to delete it. Technical logs are deleted automatically by our hosting provider, usually within 30 days.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: email us with your Discord user ID (and the server ID, if you only want it removed from one server). We'll delete it within 30 days.
  • Server data: server owners can ask us to delete all Harvest Hollow data for their server.
  • Get a copy: you can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have more rights under laws like the GDPR or CCPA. Email us to use them.

9. Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 (older in some countries). Harvest Hollow is meant for the same audience. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under that age. If you think a child under the minimum age has used Harvest Hollow, contact us and we'll delete their data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we make a big change, we'll announce it.

Questions? Email joshuarosario84@outlook.com. See also our Terms of Service.
